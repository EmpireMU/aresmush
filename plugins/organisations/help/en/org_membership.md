# Organisation Membership

Your character may belong to one or more organisations, such as noble houses, guilds, institutions, or other groups. Organisation membership is set by staff during character creation and can be updated as your character's story develops.

## Viewing Organisations

`orgs` - Lists all available organisations in the game.
`org <name>` - Shows detailed information about a specific organisation.
`orgs <character>` - Shows which organisations a character belongs to.

## Organisation Display

Your organisation memberships are displayed on your character profile, both in-game and on the web portal. Other players can see which organisations you belong to.

## Requesting Changes

If you'd like your character to join or leave an organisation as part of their storyline, contact staff via a job or page to discuss the change.
